Reader Sans Regular and Reader Sans Bold
SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1

These modified fonts use Noto Sans 2.004 for text and preserve its OpenType
layout. They include outlines from Noto Sans Math 2.001, Noto Sans Symbols
2.001, Noto Sans Symbols 2 2.003, Noto Sans CJK JP 2.004 and Noto Emoji
3.006. The CJK glyphs include JIS X 0208 level 1 kanji, common Korean
syllables and kana. Seven distinct Unicode circle emojis use a filled disc
outline so the PDF can assign their respective colors.
